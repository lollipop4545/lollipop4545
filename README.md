### 🚀 About Me

I work close to the metal — bridging **hardware, Linux kernel internals, and containerized systems**. My focus has shifted toward **low-level systems programming**: clean-room Android Linux kernel bring-up, Device Tree & driver porting, bootloader/AVB/partition engineering, and turning aging ARM64 smartphones into dedicated, ultra-low-power **Docker microservers & dashboard appliances**.

- 🎓 **Studying:** Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering at King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT).
- 🐧 **Maintaining:** **Limea Kernel** — custom container-ready Linux kernels for [Redmi Note 8 / 8T (`ginkgo`)](https://github.com/lollipop4545/Limea_Kernel-Ginkgo) and [Infinix Note 10 Pro (`X695D` / `MT6785`)](https://github.com/lollipop4545/Limea_Kernel-X695D).
- 🔧 **Hacking On:** Clean-room driver bring-up without OEM sources (MIPI DSI C-PHY displays, SPI touch, GPIO audio PAs), MediaTek BROM/Fastboot/AVB2, and Android 14 Treble GSIs.
- 🐳 **Deploying:** Native Docker Engine (`overlay2`, `cgroups`, namespaces, NAT/policy routing) on ARM64 Linux/Android targets alongside Cloudflare Zero Trust tunnels and self-hosted dashboards.
- 🛠️ **Also Building:** Full-stack control panels and web tooling with Next.js, TypeScript, and Node.js.
